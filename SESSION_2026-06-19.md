## What was done (chronological)

### 1. Stop the weekly BigQuery waste (`7947469`)
The cron re-scanned the patent target year (`today−3`) every Monday — ~162 GB/wk
of byte-identical data (patents are final once indexed). Added a **skip-existing
guard** in `refresh.py`: scan a year only if it isn't already in `patent_trend`.
Annual cron spend dropped from ~7.8 TB → ~162 GB (one scan per genuinely-new
year). Diagnosed via logs that the full cron had never actually fired the scan
(snapshot-only runs + a system-python `ModuleNotFoundError` had masked it).

### 2. One-time 2023 patent pull + site/snapshot refreshes (`a3957db`, `4e67a4a`, `671f9e0`)
Pulled 2023 patents (dry-run confirmed ~162 GB), recorded snapshots, regenerated
the static site, ran a full Gemini refresh. Resolved a recurring **push 403**:
`gh` kept reverting its active account to `joelfirenze`, which lacks write access
to `curioputterings/techno-science-observatory`. Fixed permanently with a
**repo-local credential helper** (`gh auth token --user curioputterings` at push
time) — no account-switching, no token stored in config.

### 3. The 9 → 30 domain expansion

**Decision (confirmed with the user):** proposed 30 domains, each rolling up to
one of the original 9 parents (historical continuity); staged rollout.

**Stage 1 — spine + capability (`91677b0`):**
- Rewrote `taxonomy.py` to 30 domains with anchors + `parent` + `rollup_to_parent`
  / `resolve_domain` / `PARENT_CHILDREN`.
- Extended all collector maps to 30 (`DOMAIN_CPC`, OpenAlex queries, EPO terms);
  ATS fallback now resolves a parent hint to a child.
- Cleared stale 9-domain rows; re-pulled **Gemini capability + ambition** at
  30×30 (900 cells each; two transient network failures re-pulled cleanly).
- Validated: **PCI sharpened** dramatically (Advanced Packaging / Memory /
  Nanomaterials top the ladder); proximity graph + adjacent-possible far richer.

**Stage 2 — patents + publications (`41fa518`, `fdee81a`):**
- **Batched patent query**: bucket all 30 domains into ONE table scan per year
  (conditional `COUNT(DISTINCT)` over CPC), cutting a 30-domain year from ~540 GB
  → ~18 GB. Re-pulled all 5 trend years (~90 GB). Sub-domain leaders validate
  (Taiwan tops advanced-packaging patents, Korea memory, US/CN generative-AI).
- **Publications throttling saga.** The 30×30×5 = 4,500-call burst tripped a
  sustained OpenAlex **429 penalty**. Fixes layered in:
  - real polite-pool mailto from `.env` (`OPENALEX_MAILTO`);
  - configurable pacing (`OPENALEX_PACING=3.0`) + **random jitter**
    (`OPENALEX_JITTER=1.5`) to break the rhythmic pattern;
  - **fail-fast on `URLError`** (DNS/connection drops from VPN switches) instead
    of burning the full retry budget;
  - a `--skip-existing` **retry-loop sweep** that converged over ~8 passes despite
    intermittent network outages, then a targeted pass for the last 2 stragglers.
  - Result: all 150 `(year × domain)` combos complete, 900 publication cells.

**ATS re-scrape (`413c3a3`):**

dashboard OEC tab, and the static site (new "Country complexity (Fitness)"
section — site now 9 sections). PCI kept for domains; basket complexity kept as a
complementary lens (≈orthogonal to Fitness at corr 0.07). Eigenvector ECI
retained for reference.
